Submitted Totals

Display and transport are different jobs. The <output> shows the total — a total in an editable-looking box invites editing — and a hidden input posts it. Both carry data-fs-amount-total, and the engine reconciles both at load, not just after the first edit.

Copy-To Cycle (Guard)

Each field copies to the other — a guard against runaway recursion, not a feature to build a form around.

Author-Disabled Prefilled Field (Regression)

A server-prefilled field the author disabled outright. Its value fails validation, but a disabled field never submits — it must not hold the gate.

Priced Terms

data-fs-amount with a value separates the price from the answer: a unit price multiplies a numeric answer, a choice or a file charges flat when made, and a negative amount is a discount. Bare data-fs-amount still reads the control's own value as the amount.
